## Basic Questions.

1. Display All Movies.
2. Display movie title and release year.
3. Find movies released after 2015.
4. Find Movies release between 2010 to 2020.
5. Find movies which are longer than 150 minutes.
6. Find movies with box office greater than 300 milions.
7. Display the five largest movies.
8. Display 5 hindi movies.
9. Find Users from India.
10. Find reviews with ratings of 9 or higher.
